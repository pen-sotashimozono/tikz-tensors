Expectation value after a step

An expectation value after a TEBD step: <psi(t)| O |psi(t)> with one Trotter gate shown on each side.

It is a number, so the diagram has no free index anywhere – that is the test to apply to a picture like this. The ket chain runs along the top, its conjugate along the bottom, and every physical index of the one meets the matching index of the other.

The chain is finite, so its outer bonds are one-dimensional: they carry nothing and are not drawn. An earlier draft ran a line from each end of the ket around to the matching end of the bra, which is literally the contraction but says the wrong thing – a drawn index is one with something in it, and those two have a single value each. Leaving them out is what makes the picture manifestly a scalar rather than a scalar with decoration.

Between them, read downward: the gate that evolved the ket, the operator being measured, and the gate's conjugate coming back up to the bra. The gate appears twice because the bra carries its adjoint, which is the whole reason a two-layer picture is the honest one for a measured quantity – a single layer would be an amplitude, not an expectation.

Only one site carries O, and the rest of the column is the physical index running straight through: that is what makes it a local observable.

It is a stack of five layers on five sites: the ket, the gate, the operator, the gate's adjoint, the bra. The gate spans two slots and is as wide as they are; every site is one index from the ket down to the bra, and what sits on it covers it. No height in it is chosen – the layers are one pitch apart.

The chain is drawn as plain arrays and not in a canonical form on purpose. This is the general contraction; put the state in a canonical form with its center at the measured site and most of this picture becomes the identity and disappears, which is why an expectation value is cheap there. Drawing the collapsed version would hide what collapses.

The picture

\begin{tikzpicture}
  \tnstack{E}{5}{ket, gate, op, adj, bra}
  \tnlayer{E}{ket}{5*coef/$A$}
  \tnlayer{E}{gate}{., gate/$U$/2, 2*.}
  \tnlayer{E}{op}{2*., op/$\hat O$, 2*.}
  \tnlayer{E}{adj}{., gate/$U^{\dagger}$/2, 2*.}
  \tnlayer{E}{bra}{5*coef/$\bar A$}
  \tnconnect{E}
\end{tikzpicture}
