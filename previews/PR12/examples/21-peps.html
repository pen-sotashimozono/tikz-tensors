PEPS

A projected entangled pair state on a four-by-four lattice.

One tensor per site, joined to its neighbours by the bonds of the lattice and carrying one physical index of its own, out of the plane. The lattice is drawn turned by 45 degrees, so that it reads as a plane seen from above and every physical index can go straight down without crossing a bond. The boundary is open: a tensor at the edge simply has fewer bonds.

  \tnopen{down}{P}
