Fixed points of a uniform MPS

Fixed-point equations for a uniform MPS.

Above: the transfer matrix. E is not drawn as a thing of its own, because it is not one – it is the ket tensor A and its conjugate joined along the physical index. l carries two bond indices, one into each layer, which is why it is drawn tall rather than as a site: it lives in the doubled space. Solve l E = lambda l and an infinite chain has what it needs in place of an end.

Below: the same column with the Hamiltonian's MPO tensor between the layers, so L carries three indices. The eigenvalue form is honest only block by block: an MPO for a local Hamiltonian is upper triangular with ones on the diagonal, so its transfer matrix has eigenvalue one with a Jordan block, and the block carrying the extensive part needs the energy per site subtracted and is a geometric sum rather than an eigenvector. The picture is the contraction, not the solver.

The physical index leaves each triangle from the corner of its flat side, and the site is that side's line carried on down. W sits on that line because every tensor of a site does: the triangles are set across in their slots to put the corner there, and a box is centered on it. An earlier version of this file drew both conventions side by side to see where the MPO went; on a grid the question does not arise.

Each right-hand side is a stack with no sites, the environment alone, whose indices end where the first site's tensors would be.
