Low-rank approximation

A state on five sites, and its approximation by a matrix product state of bond dimension D: the picture every MPS introduction starts from.

Psi is one tensor with five indices. Sweep through it with truncated SVDs, left to right, and each step leaves an isometry behind and carries the rest on: four left-canonical tensors and the center at the end. The truncation is where the approximately-equals comes from.

Both sides are one-layer stacks of five sites, so they share everything the package owns: Psi is one entry spanning the five slots, and is exactly as wide as the chain it is compared with; its five indices leave it at the sites' own columns, and every index on both sides ends at the same depth. Nothing in this file says any of that – it follows from both being five sites wide.
